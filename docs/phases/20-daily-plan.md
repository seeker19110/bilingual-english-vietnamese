# Phase 20 — Daily Plan

## Objective

Produce a time-bounded plan explaining why each activity is selected.

## Inputs

Goal, available time, weak skills, forgetting risk, recent activity, curriculum and SRS.

## Steps

1. Calculate mandatory reviews.
2. Rank skill-gap interventions by value.
3. Allocate time across review, teaching and production.
4. Respect learner constraints and workload limits.
5. Persist plan version and reasons.
6. Re-plan safely after interruptions.

## Tests

5/10/20-minute plans, no-review case, overloaded queue, conflicting goals and timezone boundaries.

## Acceptance

Plan is reproducible, explainable and executable through Workflow OS.
